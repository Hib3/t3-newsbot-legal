t3-newsbot Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 2026-01-05

1. Overview

t3-newsbot (the "App") is a Reddit Devvit app that periodically fetches the public Hatena Bookmark hotentry RSS feed and submits link posts to a specified subreddit. The App is provided by the creator account shown on the app directory page (the "Operator").

3. What the App does

  • Fetches a public RSS feed (title, URL, and basic metadata as provided by the feed).
  • Submits a link post to the configured subreddit, optionally applying a configured flair.
