t3-newsbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-05

1. Summary

t3-newsbot (the "App") fetches a public RSS feed and submits link posts to a configured subreddit. The App does not use analytics or trackers and is designed to minimize data handling.

2. Data the App accesses

  • Public RSS content: feed item title and URL (and any basic fields included in the public RSS). No authentication to Hatena is used.
  • Reddit data required for posting: the App interacts with Reddit to create a post and (optionally) apply a flair. This uses Reddit's platform and permissions granted during installation.

3. Data the App stores

  • Deduplication state: the App may store a short-lived record of recently posted URLs to avoid posting the same item repeatedly.
  • Configuration: the configured subreddit and scheduling/source settings.

The App does not intentionally store user-generated content, messages, IP addresses, device identifiers, or tracking identifiers.

4. Data sharing

The Operator does not sell or share personal data. Posts created by the App are public according to the subreddit and Reddit settings.

5. Security

The App runs on Reddit's Devvit infrastructure. Reasonable measures are taken to reduce stored data and limit retention.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the App evolves. The "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.

7. Contact

For questions or requests, please contact the Operator via the Reddit account listed on the app directory page.
